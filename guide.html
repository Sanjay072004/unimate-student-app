Welcome to UniMate 🎓

Your student life, organised in one place. UniMate helps you plan assignments, monitor spending and prepare listings for student essentials.

Start in three minutes

  1. Study: Open Study, enter an assignment and due date, then mark it complete when finished.
  2. Budget: Enter your monthly spending limit and add expenses to see what remains.
  3. Marketplace: Create a practice listing with a title, price and description. It stays on your device for now.

What is free?

All current beta features are free. The Premium screen shows a proposed future plan, not an active subscription. No payments are taken.

Important beta limitations

Your data is stored in your browser on this device, not in a shared account. Clearing browser storage can delete it. Use Export JSON on the main app to save a backup. Listings are not visible to other students, and there are no real ads or checkout transactions yet. Please do not enter passwords, bank details or sensitive information.
